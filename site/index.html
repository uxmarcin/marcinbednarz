About

Hi, I'm Marcin. I design software for construction, logistics and healthcare companies.

Superintendents, warehouse operators, project managers. For them a confusing screen can mean a delay, a wrong shipment or a missed approval. I enjoy the less visible parts of product design: information architecture, roles and permissions, multi-step workflows, edge cases and error states.

I work closely with product and engineering, starting with the requirements and ending with annotated specs that developers can build from. I also run uxaudyt.pl, where I audit the UX of online stores.

How I work

What I do on every project.

01

I start with real users

Before I draw anything, I try to understand how the work really happens on site and in the warehouse. I shadow users, read support tickets, talk to domain experts and look at data.

02

I share options early

I usually show two or three directions with their pros and cons. This way product and engineering decide together with me, and everybody knows the reasons.

03

I design every edge case

I design and annotate every role, state and error. Developers can build without asking for a meeting about each exception.

04

I use AI where it helps

I use Claude, OpenAI and the Figma AI agent to explore ideas, summarise research and build prototypes faster. Decisions about how people work, and the final design quality, stay with me and the team.
