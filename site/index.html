About

I am Marcin, a product designer from Rzeszów, Poland

I have worked in UX and UI since 2013. I started with the online store of Inglot, a global cosmetics brand. Then I designed websites and apps for clients at Kiwi Media and ServoCode. Since 2019 I have worked on Ingenious.Build, a US platform for construction project management. I design RFIs, Daily Logs, tasks, notifications and the home dashboard.

Most of my work has many roles and steps. In one RFI, the action banner has 33 states. Each state depends on who opens the RFI and where it is in the workflow. The idea of one banner came from the test findings of our UX researcher in the US team. I designed the states from the product requirements and those findings.

I also write notes for developers and QA. Every Daily Log screen has a UX note. It says why I made the decision and which API limit applies, if any, for example 2,000 characters for a comment. Developers and QA work from these notes.

On Vitro-Soft I was the only designer. I worked with an IVF doctor, an embryologist, a developer and the people who operate the system. I also run uxaudyt.pl, where I do UX audits of online stores.

How I work

What you can check in the case studies

I design from requirements and test results

On Ingenious.Build my input is the PRD and the test findings of our UX researcher. Every RFI state maps to a case in the requirements.

I design error states and edge cases

If a Daily Log field has an error, Save stays enabled, the message shows under the field and the typed data stays. In 4Clubbers MVP-2, a message has its own offline and failed-to-send states.

I write notes for developers and QA

Each Daily Log screen has a UX note with the reason for the decision and the API limit, if one applies. On Vitro-Soft, developers build the app from my component library.

I prepare options for a test

For BookGenius users who skip the free trial, I described three strategies and recommended an A/B test. The change was deployed. Result: +35% conversion.
