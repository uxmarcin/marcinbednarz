[02] About

Hi, I’m Marcin. I design for people who would rather be on site than in the app.

Superintendents, warehouse operators, project managers. People for whom a confusing screen means a delay, a wrong shipment or a missed approval. I like the unglamorous parts of product design: information architecture, roles and permissions, multi-step workflows, edge cases and failure states.

I work as a partner to product and engineering, from requirements to annotated specs that developers can build from. I also run uxaudyt.pl, where I audit the UX of online stores.

[03] How I work

Judgement over process.

01

Start from the field

I learn how the work happens on site and in the warehouse before I draw: shadowing, support tickets, domain experts, data.

02

Decide in the open

I put two or three directions on the table with trade-offs, so product and engineering decide with me and know why.

03

Specify to the edge case

Every role, state and failure is designed and annotated, so developers can build without a meeting for every exception.

04

Use AI where it helps

I use AI to explore options and prototype faster. Decisions about people’s work, and the final craft, stay with me and the team.
